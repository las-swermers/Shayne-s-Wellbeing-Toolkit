Why dates and diary sources matter

Record near waking when possible. You can copy a morning paper diary later. Older memories are labelled and excluded from the diary signals used to suggest habits; your results still include all saved entries with source counts. Five entries unlock an initial reflection, not a validated assessment. Aim for 7–14 mornings with school and free days represented.

How this relates to CBT-I

CBT-I is a structured treatment for insomnia that combines several approaches. This learning tool draws on sleep education and selected behavioural ideas; it is not a CBT-I treatment programme. It does not prescribe sleep restriction or reduced time in bed. Ask the health centre about appropriate support for ongoing sleep difficulties.

NHLBI: understanding CBT-I and treatment ↗
What does adolescent research show?

In a 2015 trial with 116 adolescents with insomnia, six weeks of therapist-guided CBT-I improved sleep outcomes compared with a waiting list. That supports the structured treatment studied; it does not validate this student wellbeing experiment or each habit on its own.

Support

You can ask at any point.

Talk to Shayne, your counsellor or a healthcare professional if sleep is difficult or affects your day. You do not need to finish the experiment first.

When to get help

Ongoing difficulty sleeping, strong daytime sleepiness, or worry around bedtime are worth discussing. Tell the health centre about loud snoring or waking short of breath.

What to bring

Your log can help start the conversation. A clinician can assess what is going on and whether treatment such as CBT-I would help. Ask the health centre before using sleep medicines or supplements.

Where is my information saved?

The public Lab saves to this browser. Open the connected Lab to save to your account. Someone using the same browser profile could see cached entries. Download a CSV to keep a copy. The connected Lab saves your own record to a private Sheet. Its sign-out button clears this account’s cache on this browser; Google itself stays signed in. Group reporting is being prepared.
