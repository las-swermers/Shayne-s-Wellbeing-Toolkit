Understand your options.

Every habit below links to its background. Evidence for a full treatment does not prove that one habit will work for everyone.

How this relates to CBT-I

CBT-I is a structured treatment for insomnia that combines several approaches. This learning tool draws on sleep education and selected behavioural ideas; it is not a CBT-I treatment programme. It does not prescribe sleep restriction or reduced time in bed. Ask the health centre about appropriate support for ongoing sleep difficulties.

NHLBI: understanding CBT-I and treatment ↗
What does adolescent research show?

In a 2015 trial with 116 adolescents with insomnia, six weeks of therapist-guided CBT-I improved sleep outcomes compared with a waiting list. That supports the structured treatment studied; it does not validate this two-week school experiment or each habit on its own.

Support

You can ask at any point.

Talk to Shayne, your counsellor or a healthcare professional if sleep is difficult or affects your day. You do not need to finish the experiment first.

When to get help

Ongoing difficulty sleeping, strong daytime sleepiness, or worry around bedtime are worth discussing. Tell the health centre about loud snoring or waking short of breath.

What to bring

Your log can help start the conversation. A clinician can assess what is going on and whether treatment such as CBT-I would help. Ask the health centre before using sleep medicines or supplements.

Where is my information saved?

For now, entries are saved in this browser, not your Google account or a shared Sheet. Someone using the same browser profile could see them. Download a CSV to keep a copy.
