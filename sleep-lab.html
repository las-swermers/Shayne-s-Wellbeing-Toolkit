Your body clock moved later.

Around puberty the body clock shifts one to two hours later. You are running a body that wants to fall asleep late, inside a building that starts at seven.

What a short night actually costs

Drag the slider

A night is four or five cycles. Deep sleep, which does the physical repair, loads into the early ones. Dream sleep, which does most of the memory and emotional work, loads into the last ones. A short night removes the end; it does not trim the whole evenly.

      08 · More help

      When to talk to someone.

      Self-help covers the ordinary version of bad sleep, which is most of it. Some things need a person, and going early is easier than going late.

      Come and talk if any of these are true

      • Falling asleep takes more than half an hour most nights, and has done for three weeks or more.
      • You wake in the night and cannot get back several nights a week.
      • You dread going to bed, or the hours before lights out are the worst part of your day.
      • You sleep a full night and are still exhausted all day, every day.
      • You snore heavily, wake gasping, or someone has told you that you stop breathing.
      • Nightmares or flashbacks are waking you regularly.
      • Your legs feel restless or crawling in the evening.
      • You are using anything — supplements, medication, alcohol, vapes — to get to sleep or stay awake.
      • Your mood, appetite or interest in things has changed alongside the sleep.

      What happens if you come in

      The treatment with the best evidence for teenagers is CBT for insomnia — the structured version of what this toolkit borrows from. A few sessions, no medication, and it works better than sleep education alone. You can ask for it by name.

      On melatonin and supplements

      Melatonin shifts your body clock; it does not sedate you. It only helps at the right hour and dose for your clock, and the large supermarket doses are usually wrong. Talk to the health centre before ordering anything. If you are already taking something, say so — we need to know, and you are not in trouble.

      If it is not really about sleep

      Sleep is often the first thing to go when something else is wrong: homesickness, a relationship, results, something at home you are carrying alone at eleven at night. When the problem is a thing and not a habit, these tools will only get you so far. Come and talk about the thing.
