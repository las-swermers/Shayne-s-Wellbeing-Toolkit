Sleep Lab

For 14 nights, notice what your sleep is already doing—then test two or three small changes. You are not trying to become perfect. You are looking for useful evidence about your week.

What we know: teenagers generally need 8–10 hours, and many body clocks run later. Morning light, moving a phone out of bed, and getting up briefly if you have been awake for about 20 minutes can help. If you are thinking about melatonin or have ongoing sleep worries, speak with the health centre—this page does not give dose advice.
